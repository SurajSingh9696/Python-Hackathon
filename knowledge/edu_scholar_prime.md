# Scholar Prime Education Loan

## Overview
Scholar Prime is designed for students pursuing undergraduate and postgraduate degrees in recognized Indian universities and colleges.

## Key Financial Features
- **Loan Amount**: ₹50,000 to ₹20,00,000 (₹20 Lakh).
- **Interest Rate**: 9.5% to 11.0% per annum (floating reducing-balance).
- **Repayment Tenure**: Up to 7 years (84 months) following the moratorium.
- **Moratorium Period**: Course duration plus 6 months (up to 24 months maximum). During moratorium, simple interest is charged.
- **Processing Fee**: 1.0% of loan amount + 18% GST.
- **Collateral**: No collateral required up to ₹20 Lakh.
- **Co-applicant**: Mandatory parent, guardian, or earning sibling as co-borrower.

## Eligibility
1. Indian citizen with confirmed admission letter into an accredited higher education institution.
2. Co-applicant must have steady monthly take-home income with FOIR under 50%.
3. Minimum student age 18 years at time of disbursement.

## Required Documents
- Valid Admission Offer Letter with Course Fee Schedule.
- Co-applicant Income Proof (Salary slips last 3 months or Form 16 / ITR).
- KYC Documents (Aadhaar Card, PAN Card) of student and co-applicant.
- 6 months bank statement of the salary/operating account.
