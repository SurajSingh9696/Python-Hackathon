# Prepayment and Foreclosure Guidelines

## What is Part-Prepayment?
Part-prepayment is paying a lump sum amount towards your principal outstanding before the tenure ends. This reduces either your monthly EMI or your remaining loan duration.

## What is Foreclosure?
Foreclosure (full prepayment) is paying off the entire remaining loan balance in a single payment before the scheduled term completes.

## Are there Prepayment or Foreclosure Penalties?
- **Floating Rate Loans to Individual Borrowers**: As per Reserve Bank of India (RBI) regulatory directives, banks and NBFCs cannot charge any foreclosure charges or prepayment penalties on floating rate term loans sanctioned to individual borrowers for purposes other than business.
- **Fixed Rate Loans**: May attract a nominal prepayment fee (typically 1% to 2% plus GST on the prepaid amount) as stipulated in the loan sanction agreement.
- **Minimum Notice Period**: Borrowers usually need to notify the lender 7 to 15 business days prior to initiating foreclosure.
