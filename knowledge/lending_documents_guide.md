# Loan Verification & Documentation Checklist

## Mandatory Document Categories

### 1. Identity & Address Proof (KYC)
- **Primary**: Aadhaar Card (with masked first 8 digits), PAN Card, or Passport.
- **Address Verification**: Electricity bill, LPG connection receipt, or rent agreement (not older than 3 months) if address differs from Aadhaar.

### 2. Proof of Income
- **Salaried Individuals**: Last 3 months payslips, Form 16 (Part A & B), and 6 months bank statement showing employer salary credits.
- **Self-Employed / Business**: 2 years ITR with computation, Profit & Loss statement, and 12 months operating current account statement.

### 3. Education Specific Documentation
- **Admission Offer**: Unconditional or conditional letter of admission on official college letterhead.
- **Fee Structure**: Breakdown of tuition, hostel charges, examination fees, and living cost estimates provided by the institution.
