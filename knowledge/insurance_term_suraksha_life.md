# Suraksha Pure Term Life

## Overview
Pure risk life protection plan offering substantial financial security for your family in the event of untimely demise of the breadwinner.

## Key Features
- **Sum Assured**: ₹50 Lakh to ₹2 Crore.
- **Tenure**: Up to age 75 or term between 10 to 40 years.
- **Payout Options**: Lump sum, monthly income, or combination (50% lump sum + 50% monthly income for 10 years).
- **Riders**: Optional Accelerated Critical Illness Rider covering 36 critical conditions, and Accidental Death Benefit Rider.
- **Tax Benefit**: Premium paid qualifies for deduction under Section 80C, and claim payouts are 100% tax-exempt under Section 10(10D).

## Eligibility
- Entry Age: 18 to 65 years.
- Minimum Annual Income: ₹3,00,000.
- Mandatory medical checkup for cover over ₹1 Crore or age above 45.
