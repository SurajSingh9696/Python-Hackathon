# Arogya Shield Health Plan

## Overview
A comprehensive health insurance policy covering individual and family floater hospitalizations with zero co-payment and cashless hospital network coverage.

## Key Features
- **Sum Insured**: ₹5 Lakh to ₹25 Lakh.
- **Initial Waiting Period**: 30 days from policy inception (accidental injuries covered immediately from day 1).
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period**: 36 months of continuous coverage.
- **Co-Payment**: 0% (zero co-pay across all approved network hospitals).
- **Room Rent**: No room rent sub-limit on single private standard air-conditioned rooms.
- **Pre and Post Hospitalization**: 60 days pre-hospitalization and 180 days post-hospitalization medical expenses covered.
- **Network Hospitals**: Over 10,000+ empaneled cashless hospitals across India.

## Eligibility
- Proposer age: 18 to 65 years. Children eligible from 91 days up to 25 years.
- Annual cumulative bonus (No Claim Bonus) of 10% per claim-free year up to 100% of sum insured.
