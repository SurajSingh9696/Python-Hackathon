# Merit Premier Student Line

## Overview
Merit Premier offers high-ticket educational credit at concessional interest rates for premier national and international academic institutes (e.g., IIT, IIM, AIIMS, top global universities).

## Key Financial Features
- **Loan Amount**: ₹1,00,000 to ₹40,00,000 (₹40 Lakh).
- **Interest Rate**: 9.0% to 10.0% per annum.
- **Repayment Tenure**: Up to 10 years (120 months) after moratorium.
- **Moratorium Period**: Up to 36 months (study duration plus 1 year). Simple interest accrual.
- **Processing Fee**: Concessional 0.75% + 18% GST.
- **Collateral**: Tangible collateral required for amounts exceeding ₹7.5 Lakh (residential property, fixed deposits, or government bonds).
- **Co-applicant**: Mandatory co-borrower.

## Eligibility & Tax Benefits
1. Admission into top-tier NIRF or QS-ranked institutions.
2. Full interest deduction benefit under Section 80E of Income Tax Act for up to 8 years.
3. Maximum FOIR allowance up to 55%.

## Required Documents
- Official Admission confirmation and fee structure.
- Co-applicant 2-year ITR, computation of income, and salary slips.
- Collateral property papers / FDR receipts.
- Student academic records and entrance exam scorecard.
