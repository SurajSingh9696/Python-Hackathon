# Lending Eligibility & FOIR Framework

## Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio (FOIR)
FOIR represents the percentage of an applicant's net monthly income committed to paying debt obligations (existing EMIs plus proposed new EMI).

$$\text{FOIR} = \frac{\text{Existing Obligations} + \text{New EMI}}{\text{Net Monthly Take-home Income}} \times 100$$

### Thresholds
- **Comfortable Band (≤ 35%)**: High disposable buffer. Repayment is smooth with low default probability.
- **Stretched Band (36% to 50%)**: Moderate buffer. Borrower can service loan but must monitor discretionary spends.
- **High Risk / Unaffordable (> 50%)**: Exceeds standard responsible lending thresholds. Applications in this band require debt consolidation, co-applicant addition, or loan tenor elongation.

## General Criteria
1. **Age**: Minimum 18 years for students (with earning co-applicant), minimum 21 years for primary salaried/business borrowers; maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
2. **Income Proof**: Verifiable bank salary credits or audited Form 16 / ITR. Cash or unverifiable income is not counted towards FOIR capacity.
3. **Co-applicant Guidelines**: For students with zero income, a co-applicant with regular income is mandatory. The co-applicant's income is used for FOIR evaluation.
