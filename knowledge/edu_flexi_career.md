# EduFlex Quick Career Loan

## Overview
EduFlex is tailored for working professionals, upskilling certifications, bootcamps, and executive diplomas where immediate repayment is viable.

## Key Financial Features
- **Loan Amount**: ₹25,000 to ₹10,00,000 (₹10 Lakh).
- **Interest Rate**: 10.5% to 12.0% per annum (reducing-balance).
- **Repayment Tenure**: 12 to 48 months.
- **Moratorium Period**: None. Repayment of principal and interest begins immediately the month following disbursement.
- **Processing Fee**: 1.5% + 18% GST.
- **Collateral & Co-applicant**: Not required if applicant meets income threshold.

## Eligibility
1. Salaried or self-employed individuals with minimum monthly take-home of ₹20,000.
2. Minimum work experience of 6 months.
3. Total fixed obligations including new EMI must not exceed 45% of net income (FOIR ≤ 45%).

## Required Documents
- Salary slips for the last 3 months or bank statement reflecting salary credits.
- PAN Card and Aadhaar Card for digital e-KYC.
- Course enrollment invoice or certification admission proof.
