# Care Plus Comprehensive Health

## Overview
A high-cover healthcare plan designed for larger families and senior dependents featuring accelerated 24-month pre-existing condition clearance and global emergency assist.

## Key Features
- **Sum Insured**: ₹10 Lakh to ₹50 Lakh.
- **Initial Waiting Period**: 30 days (accidental hospitalizations covered from day 1).
- **Pre-Existing Disease (PED) Waiting Period**: 24 months (accelerated clearance).
- **Co-Payment**: 10% co-payment on non-network or voluntary hospitalizations; 0% at primary tier-1 centers.
- **Modern Treatments**: Robotic surgeries, stem cell therapy, and oral chemotherapy covered up to full sum insured.
- **Maternity & Newborn**: Optional rider covering up to ₹75,000 after 24 months waiting period.

## Eligibility
- Adult entry age: 18 to 75 years with lifelong renewability.
